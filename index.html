Achievements

Tools I built for my plant

APP · Wagon Tippler

Leave Tracker App

A simple leave management app to track leave of casual workers and staff in the Wagon Tippler department.

What it does

  • Admins can add, edit and delete leave records.
  • Shows monthly leave summaries and exports data to Excel.
  • Has a calendar view and a home-screen widget to see who is available and how many people are on leave today.
  • Cuts manual tracking and helps manpower planning.

Why I built it

  • WhatsApp leave messages get lost and are hard to find later.
  • See today’s leave count and names instantly.
  • Check future leave early to avoid manpower shortage.
  • Plan daily duty without reading old group chats.
APP · OT & Attendance

Shift Planner

An app to track overtime (OT) and attendance in one place, without manual calculations.

Key features

  • Tracks total OT hours automatically.
  • Graphs to compare OT across people.
  • Filters for current week, month or year.
  • Records double-duty and 12-hour duties.
  • Group-wise and section-wise attendance, with editable records.
  • PDF export and one-click data reset.

Why I built it

  • OT was kept in Excel with manual entry. Checking records like A+B or half-shift OT took a lot of time.
  • Now shift in-charges record OT, update attendance and track casual OT and total hours in one place.
  • Only authorized shift in-charges can use it.
  • Very useful in shutdowns and breakdowns, when OT and manpower needs are high.
WEBSITE · Knowledge Platform

PelletMech

A website that brings pellet plant maintenance and process knowledge together in one place, from basic to advanced topics.

Key features

  • Pellet plant maintenance knowledge and guides.
  • Process-related technical information.
  • Basic to advanced learning resources.
  • Advanced engineering formulas and calculations.

Why I built it

  • New trainees often find it hard to find the right information on maintenance, safety, IMS, electrical basics, SAP and process operations.
  • Useful for foremen, fitters, welders, housekeeping staff and maintenance people in their daily work.
  • Also helps with interview preparation and revising technical topics.
